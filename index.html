Our firm

Trueworth is built on a simple principle: the right advice, given plainly, at the moment it counts.

We act for businesses, families and individuals who want a lawyer who understands what is at stake and says what they think. Every matter is led by a partner from first call to final signature.

    Partner-led

How we work

A straightforward way of working

  1. Listen

    We start by understanding the people, the commercial picture and what a good outcome looks like for you.

  2. Advise

    You get a clear view of your options, the risks and our recommendation, in writing.

  3. Act

    We do the work to an agreed scope and timetable, and keep you updated without being chased.

  4. Stay close

    When the matter closes, we stay available for the next question, whenever it comes.
